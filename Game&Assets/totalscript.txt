INTRODUCTION
Welcome to Cinco Noches con Alfredo!

BACKSTORY
On a stormy December night, you meet Alfredo, the owner of Hotel de Español, at a bar. He offers shelter from the storm. Intrigued (or desperate), you follow him.

NIGHT 1 – ARRIVAL
You arive at the hotel and enter.
Alfredo leaves to fetch keys for a hotelroom for you. The lobby is eerily quiet.

Do you whant to *Wait* or *Explore*


Wait:
You decide to wait in the lobby

Alfredo returns, leading you to Room 213 a bare room with a single bed. Unease gnaws at you.

Explore:
Your curious nature makes you want to explore the lobby

You find a shifting painting and a guestbook with Alfredo’s decades-old signature. He calls you back sharply.
You both walk toward the room 213 and he hands you the keys to open the door. When you do so a wave of fear hits you.



Choice 2: 
Standing outside the room scared, your not sure what to do.

Do you whant to *Trust* Alfredo or Try to *leave*


Trust Alfredo: 
You trust Alfredos good intentions and wave him goodbye before you go to sleep. 

The soothing sound of the rain lulls you to sleep.

Try to Leave:
"I just remembered i have something important to do tomorrow so i actually think it is best for me to go home".

Alfredo smiles friendly and answers "I completely understand, hope you get home safe"

After you left the building and started walking down the street you hear a weird noise near by and it frightens you.

Do you still whant to *leave* or go back to the *hotel*

leave:
You choose to ignore the fear and keeps on walking. As the sound gets louder and louder you are convinced it is the sound of foot steps behind you. 

You start running You run faster but still the steps are behind you. Running in the dark of the night you missed a step and tripped. Lying face down on the sidewalk you then turn around to get up. 
Standing over you you see a large darg figure, before have chance to react everything goes black.

Hotel:
You walk back to the hotel and lie to Alfredo about how you really do not think you will be able to get home in this storm.

Tired and wet you return to the room and fall into an uneasy sleep...

NIGHT 2 – BREAKFAST & CLUES
Scene: 
You wake up and walk down to the Dining Hall.
Alfredo serves breakfast, mentioning the hotel’s “slowly revealed truths.” A locked door looms behind him.
Choice 1:

Ask About the Door: He deflects. Later, you hear muffled screams in the halls.

Explore Alone: Find a hidden room with news clippings: “Guests Disappear Without a Trace.”

NIGHT 3 – DINNER & DECEPTION
Scene: Alfredo’s Invitation
Alfredo insists on a candlelit dinner. His demeanor feels possessive.
Choice 1:

Accept Dinner: Notice a red smear on his wine glass. He dismisses it. Afterward, tortured screams echo nearby.

Refuse and Flee: The storm traps you. You awaken bruised, back in your room.

NIGHT 4 – THE MAZE
Scene: Shifting Corridors
The hotel warps into a labyrinth. Alfredo beckons to a “private parlor” filled with portraits of missing guests.
Choice 1:

Follow Him: He cryptically hints at the hotel’s dark past. Find a blood-smeared journal: “They are mine forever.”

Search for Exits: Halls bleed. A shadowy figure drags a victim past a window. Alfredo whispers: “Stay.”

NIGHT 5 – FINAL CONFRONTATION
Scene: The Heart of Darkness
The hotel pulses with malice. Alfredo, now unhinged, declares: “You belong here.”
Choice 1:

Confront Him: Fight to activate a hidden switch. Escape briefly—but the cycle resets.

Flee Through Secret Passages: Find an “EXIT,” but Alfredo corners you. The storm swallows your screams.

THE END
You survive… but the hotel’s grip lingers. Will Alfredo ever let you go?
